KEEP THESE CLOSE 常用表达

01

Could you show me what you mean?您能示范一下您的意思吗？

02

What are you noticing in my child's movement?您观察到我孩子的动作有什么特点？

03

Where should I place my hands?我的手应该放在哪里？

04

What should I look for when we practice at home?我们在家练习时应该观察什么？
